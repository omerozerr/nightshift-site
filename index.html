Nightshift

A personal, self-hosted AI assistant.

Nightshift is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own computer and is used through a Telegram chat. It can run tasks, keep notes, set reminders and, when the owner connects their Google account, search, read and send email on their behalf. Every outgoing email needs the owner's explicit approval in the chat before it is sent.

Nightshift is not a commercial service and has no public sign-up. Each person who uses it runs it themselves and connects only their own accounts.
