Privacy policy

Nightshift · last updated 29 October 2026

Nightshift is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant. It runs on its user's own computer, for that user only. This policy explains what Nightshift does with data from your Google account when you connect it.

What Google data Nightshift accesses

  • Your email address, to show which Google account is connected.
  • Your Gmail messages (read-only access), only when you ask Nightshift to search or read your email.
  • Sending email: Nightshift sends an email only after you approve that specific email in the chat.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to carry out the request you made, for example to answer "do I have unread emails?" or to send a reply you approved. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

Everything is stored on the computer that runs Nightshift, not on any Nightshift server (there is none). The long-term Google access token is stored encrypted. Email content that Nightshift reads to answer a request is kept in the local task history on that computer, so you can review what the assistant did. You can delete it at any time.

Who the data is shared with

To answer a request, the relevant email content is sent to the AI model provider that Nightshift is configured to use (such as DeepSeek or Anthropic), under that provider's API terms. It is shared with no one else.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google at any time on Nightshift's Connections page, which also revokes Nightshift's access at Google. You can also remove access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Nightshift's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
